Kharcha – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Kharcha ("the app") helps you track your own expenses, budgets, EMIs/installments and Udhar.

What we access

  • Bank SMS: on Android, only if you allow SMS access, Kharcha reads bank/UPI/card SMS from your inbox on the phone (personal numbers, OTPs and offers are skipped). You can also paste SMS instead. Each SMS is parsed on your device and only the extracted fields — amount, debit/credit, merchant name, payment mode, last 4 digits of account, date — are saved. The pasted text itself is not stored.
  • Email address or mobile number: used only to sign you in with a one-time password (OTP).
  • Data you enter: expenses, budgets, categories, EMIs/installments, Udhar entries and settings.

Where data is stored

Your data is stored in your private account in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), protected by security rules so only you can read or write it. A copy is cached on your device for offline use.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. No ads, no analytics SDKs.

Your controls

  • Export all data (Customize → Backup) or delete it (Customize → Delete all data).
  • Delete your account any time: Customize → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and all its data.
